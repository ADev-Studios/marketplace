How It Works

Lift

Thermals keep you up

Ride rising air currents to stay aloft. Miss them and the void claims you.

Zone

Stay in the middle zone

The safe area shrinks. Drift too far and you’re out of the fight.

Survival

Last one in the middle wins

Shoot, bump, or out-fly your friends. Only one glider remains.

Controls

  • W A S D
    Steer Bank and turn your hang glider
  • Shift
    Dive Drop altitude fast to pick up speed or dodge
  • Mouse
    Aim Look around and line up your shot
  • LMB
    Shoot Fire a ball at another glider
